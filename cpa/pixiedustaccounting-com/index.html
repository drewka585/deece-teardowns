3 gaps I'd close first · tap to expand
New clients ride on referrals you cannot turn up on demand▼
What's there now
Word of mouth and repeat clients bring in good, well-fit business. That is a sign the work and the relationships are strong.
What it's costing
Growth is capped by whoever happens to refer you this quarter. Your payroll, monthly books, and tax services can serve far more owners than the referral stream delivers, so the calendar stays lumpy and hard to plan around.
Quick fix
Add one repeatable outbound channel that points ideal small-business owners in your favorite niches straight at the services page. It runs in the background so growth stops depending on this month's referrals.
The ready owner has no way to book on the spot▼
What's there now
A clear services page and a contact form for anyone who wants to reach out.
What it's costing
An owner who is ready to talk has to fill a form and then wait for a reply, and your stated hours are 9 to 2, so an evening or weekend visitor cools off before you can respond. Every ready visitor without a visible way to grab a time quietly leaks people who were one click from a conversation.
Quick fix
One consistent book-a-call step with a short intake on the services and contact pages, so interest turns into a booked call the moment it peaks instead of an hour later when the tab is closed.
Invisible where owners first look, and no way to follow up▼
What's there now
Strong national-feeling positioning, transparent pricing, and an email capture popup already in place.
What it's costing
When an Orange County owner searches for an accountant, the local pack is owned by firms with review moats, CalPro at 132 reviews, California CPA Corner at 98. Pixie Dust is not in that list. And with no analytics or retargeting pixel on the site, every visitor who leaves without acting is gone for good, with no way to measure what is working or gently follow up.
Quick fix
Claim and fill the Google Business Profile, add a simple review ask to your happiest current clients, and drop a basic analytics and retargeting pixel in place so visitors can be understood and reached again. Low effort, and it compounds every month once running.
